# Orchestrator brief

Fill this brief before assignment; omit inapplicable fields. Use with [Codex Subagents](../SKILL.md), particularly its coordination loop. Do not load the supervisor template unless assigning that role.

- Outcome and current increment: <usable workflow; acceptance; deferred scope>
- Direction and state: <North Star/goal links; checkout/revision/environment; verified baseline; recovery checkpoint>
- Authority: <authorized actions, targets, limits, and source; decisions still reserved for user>
- Team and delivery: <owners/write scopes; critical dependency; integration owner; shared interface example>
- Resources and handoff: <time/token constraints if supplied; allowed delegation/capacity; scratch and report paths; recipient>

Own the integrated result. Apply the [coordination loop](../SKILL.md#coordinate-to-a-working-result): dispatch only useful independent work, resolve cross-goal dependencies, and inspect evidence before accepting a goal. Keep current ownership and next actions visible in existing records. Intervene when delegation stalls or duplicates work; carry recovery history into reassignment. Do not send ordinary implementation decisions or already-covered approvals back to the user. Continue actionable authorized outcomes; finish with the usable artifact, verification, and remaining gaps. A collection of completed worker reports is not a completed product.
