## Outcome and acceptance

- **Ask:** full requested outcome, user/workflow/environment, authorized scope changes and their source.
- **Acceptance:** observable required criteria and evidence; constraints, resources and protected data/behavior.
- **Remaining:** one current commitment, required work after it, and optional/deferred scope. Finish before expanding.
- **Authority:** one existing commander as approval owner, standing grant/source, targets/actions, subdelegation and expressly retained human decisions. Covered actions need no reapproval.

## Ownership and tasks

- Builder, exact write scope/task scratch, first artifact/action, dependencies and assigned report path.
- Nonauthor reviewer, actual review scope and integration owner. Compatible roles may share an agent; the author cannot certify their own work.
- For delegated work: verified route/tool setup, context/validation command and parent-enforced checkpoint/deadline. Use relevant companion guidance rather than copying it here.

## Execution record

- Current artifact/revision and actual checks/results; link evidence once.
- Next action and why its expected benefit warrants total cost; stop optional work at diminishing returns.
- If stalled: unmet criterion, attempts/ruled-out paths, preserved diff, remaining recovery allowance, next distinguishing check or exact unblock condition.
- Record only material decisions and lessons that change future action. Keep operating owner/signals/cadence only when ongoing operations were requested.

## Acceptance

- Reconcile each required outcome: passed, unmet or unverified, with current evidence and one independent verdict.
- Approval owner records the decision once: deliver the accepted increment/full endpoint, repair a failed criterion, or retain an exact dependency. Do not mark the full goal complete while required outcomes remain.
- Handoff the usable result, checks and material limits. Update the existing index where required; no duplicate approval or learning record.
