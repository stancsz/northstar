# Operating principles across domains

Use this reference when choosing or reviewing coordination under uncertainty. Apply the [core focus rules](../SKILL.md#focus-effort-before-expanding-work), authority and recovery rules; use the [operating system](operating-system.md) to turn them into decisions, useful ceremonies and operational learning. This is a selective comparison of stated practices, not a ranking of organizations, a universal recipe, or evidence that borrowing their language improves AI agents.

## Shared patterns and engineering adaptations

Research checked on 2026-09-29. Sources describe different environments: military coordination, production quality, large-company decisions and early product discovery. The patterns below overlap across parts of this sample; they are not all present in every source. Organizational self-descriptions, doctrine and investor advice are not controlled performance studies, and successful examples can conceal selection bias.

| Pattern and source basis | Northstar decision | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Concentrate effort: Sunzi, *Xu Shi* (虛實); USMC *Warfighting*, Main Effort. | Prioritize the condition limiting the next usable delivery; assign supporting work for a concrete contribution and change priorities with evidence. | Do not concentrate writers on the same files or confuse easy task counts with delivery. |
| Shared intent and local initiative: Sunzi, *Mou Gong* (謀攻); US Army mission command; PLA Daily's discussion of frontline autonomous coordination. | Give owners the intended effect, current evidence and authority; let them coordinate methods directly. Reuse existing roles and source-of-truth records. | The PLA article is a public professional discussion, not proof of universal practice. Direct coordination does not transfer file ownership or grant external-action authority. |
| Fast reversible decisions: Amazon's 2016 shareholder letter. | Hear material objections, then let the accountable owner decide within authority; execute and correct when evidence changes. | Do not demand unanimity or copy a fixed information percentage. Irreversible consequences need proportionate scrutiny; dissent about actual defects or authority remains actionable. |
| Small commitments with real feedback: YC's MVP teaching and Sequoia's customer/problem framing. | Test the assumption that changes the next commitment using the smallest informative authorized workflow or user check. Compare learning/useful outcome with total cost and downside. | Our staged-commitment rule is an adaptation. Do not confuse a demo with demand, expand beyond authorization, copy a VC portfolio's loss tolerance, or invent ROI scores. |
| Quality at the source and limited unfinished work: Toyota Production System. | Withhold defective inputs from downstream acceptance, repair at the owner, and favor completing a usable flow over producing more disconnected parts. | Contain affected work only. Do not stop unrelated services, let reviewers seize implementation authority, or treat preferences as defects. |
| Capacity and honest learning: Sunzi, *Zuo Zhan* (作戰); USMC *Tactics* on reserves and *Warfighting* on initiative and mistakes. | Leave capacity for integration/verification/repair; preserve useful failed-experiment evidence without automatically adding approval layers. | No fixed reserve percentage or permanent idle agent. A failed experiment is not accepted delivery; unchanged failures, concealment and unauthorized actions require intervention. |

Direct fetches of the cited military pages/PDFs were unavailable; relevant indexed official passages, not complete publications, were inspected. Business pages and Sunzi text were accessible. These sources justify attribution only; proposed applications still need real-task evaluation. Modern US practices are not attributed to the founding generation, and shared ideas are not claimed as exclusive national inventions.

## Concrete applications

- **Many completed components, no usable export:** prioritize end-to-end export. One owner integrates; independent workers check relevant format/error cases. Defer optional themes and inspect the actual export before acceptance.
- **Uncertain API capability:** check the capability cheaply in an authorized environment before building a large integration. Use a supported equivalent only if it meets the same contract; otherwise retain the blocker and advance independent work. A fake service cannot verify the real integration.
- **Two competent agents disagree about a reversible internal design:** inspect the decisive objection, then the assigned owner chooses within the existing contract. Both follow that decision; reopen for relevant new evidence or a failed criterion. Neither consensus nor an extra management tier is required.
- **A producer delivers corrupt output:** the consumer flags it and stops treating it as verified. The owner repairs and reruns the failing check; unrelated safe work continues. A reviewer does not shut down shared infrastructure.
- **A useful experiment fails near a deadline:** preserve evidence and the verified baseline. Spend remaining capacity on the necessary repair and checks, not optional branches. Apply existing exhausted-recovery limits; do not relabel a failed experiment as delivery success.

Reuse existing intent, authority, recovery, QA and learning records. Do not add an OODA board, campaign headquarters, permanent red team, fixed team-size slogan or recurring approval ceremony merely to resemble an admired institution. Retain required alignment, debate, expert consultation and learning reviews with concrete outputs. Transfer practices that improve a concrete decision and can be inspected.
