# Functional agent profiles

Choose functions from the task's unmet decisions and risks. These are reusable responsibilities, not new skill identities, ranks, or required headcount. They complement the [orchestrator/supervisor/worker structure](../SKILL.md#roles-and-authority): hierarchy says who owns and reports the work; function says what expertise and decision each assignment supplies. Keep the smallest team that covers the work. One agent can combine compatible functions when capability and context fit, but never create or own a deliverable and independently certify it. Review independence follows the artifact and its creators, not the agent label or context: an author reopening a fresh context is still not independent.

## Combine or separate

Combine product framing with architecture for a small, coherent task when one capable agent can preserve the user's acceptance while making the technical choices. Combine architecture with implementation when interfaces are local and reversible. An operator may also build a small internal tool; another agent must judge its readiness and independently audit the operational decisions or claims being reviewed. Combine experience inspection with QA when the same uninvolved reviewer has the relevant visual or domain skill and sees the original criteria; no separate taste agent is required.

Use a separate reviewer agent or context when independent checking is needed; it must not be the artifact creator, even if the author starts a fresh context. The task brief names the creator(s), independent reviewer, accountable decision owner, and the actual artifact/revision under review. QA may operate an isolated test setup or exercise the real service; it must not own creation/configuration or the operational decision being audited. A compatible manager may record an independent reviewer's verdict, but cannot supply or override its own QA approval. A manager may integrate and repair within its ownership, but those repairs are not independent review; recheck changed scope through an uninvolved reviewer. If nobody independent is available, record the result as independently unverified and state the exact evidence or reviewer needed. Do not silently substitute self-review or weaken original criteria.

Functional roles do not replace accountability. The orchestrator owns integrated outcome and direction; supervisors own their goal and acceptance record; workers own assigned deliverables and handoffs. Preserve owner mandates and authorization through transfers. Give dissent with evidence to the responsible superior; unresolved material disagreement uses the existing decision-meeting practice. At meaningful checkpoints carry forward expected versus observed results, rejected paths, current recovery state, decision owner and next action. These profiles are a practical starting design for this package, not a universally proven optimal taxonomy.
