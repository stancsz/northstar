# Agent-to-agent handoffs

Use this practice for delegated work, peer coordination and cross-system handoffs. It is a Markdown operating agreement, not an A2A client/server implementation. Use available authorized tools; a role profile or advertised capability grants no permission to contact, send data, spend or deploy.

## Assign, exchange, accept

1. **Match the function and check conflicts.** Use [role profiles](role-profiles.md), actual capability and access. Name creator and accountable decision owner; name a separate reviewer when independent acceptance is required. Give that reviewer original intent and access to evidence, not only the builder's summary. Share the minimum relevant context; never credentials or unrelated private data.
2. **Identify the work once.** Carry the existing goal/task link, requested outcome, fixed criteria, artifact/path and revision, owned write scope, dependencies, authority, resource limits and next check. Preserve applicable lessons, rejected paths and remaining recovery. A short peer clarification needs no new document or task ceremony.
3. **Make state actionable.** Report working, handed off, waiting for specific input/authority, failed or canceled, with evidence and the next responsible action. A worker's completed message means its output is available for review, not accepted. Silence or an observation timeout does not prove completion or failure; inspect the known live handle before redispatching. A stale message cannot overwrite a newer artifact or acceptance record.
4. **Transfer ownership explicitly.** Peers may resolve interfaces inside their mandates. Changes to shared contracts go to affected owners; one editor owns each shared artifact. Stop or confirm completion of the previous writer before transferring its scope. Repeated delivery of a handoff must not duplicate work or side effects. Reassignment preserves failed-attempt counts; it does not manufacture a new recovery budget.
5. **Inspect before accepting.** When required, a nonauthor checks the artifact and criteria; otherwise the commander accepts from direct evidence. Record accepted, returned with a reproduced failure, or unverified. Review the repaired revision; a reviewer who repairs cannot recheck it. Transport success alone is insufficient.
6. **Resolve and learn.** Route an unresolved material decision to the existing approval owner; use the shortest evidence exchange that can resolve it. Preserve positions and limits; no vote can waive failed acceptance or expand authority. Record a changed decision or reusable lesson once and carry it into the next assignment.

## When an actual A2A transport is used

The [official specification](https://a2a-protocol.org/latest/specification/) describes capability discovery and separate authentication/authorization, plus messages, tasks and artifacts. Its task lifecycle distinguishes interrupted states from terminal outcomes. The [task lifecycle guide](https://a2a-protocol.org/latest/topics/life-of-a-task/) explains context continuity and that terminal tasks cannot restart; subsequent refinement uses a linked new task. Sources checked 2026-09-29.

Respect the host's actual protocol/version. Preserve its task/context/artifact identifiers in the existing handoff where available. A linked new transport task still inherits Northstar's goal, acceptance and recovery history; a new ID is not a fresh attempt allowance. Keep transport lifecycle and our acceptance decision distinct. Profiles, recusal, side-effect deduplication and the review workflow above are Northstar requirements, not guarantees supplied by A2A or a claim of protocol conformance.
