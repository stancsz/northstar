# GDE integration

In Goal-Driven Engineering, product intent and major architecture are normally `CHALLENGE` or `HUMAN_ONLY`; early product exploration is `COCREATE`; explicit sandbox code and tests are `AUTO`; production deployment and migrations are `GUARD`; incidents and strategy changes are `CHALLENGE`.

Put the collaboration contract in `GOAL.md` after the steward-owned acceptance criteria. Builder progress can change evidence and can escalate the contract. It cannot weaken acceptance, alter commit authority, widen bounds, or infer approval from a timeout.
