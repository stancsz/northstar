# Routing rubric

Risk flags are OR conditions. Complexity flags need two positive signals because open work alone is not necessarily dangerous. Missing `risk` or `complexity` evidence becomes high/high `CHALLENGE`, because unknown is not evidence of safety.

Do not treat model capability, a passing smoke test, or a prior success as authority to increase autonomy. A request to expand permissions, scope, budget, data access, or external impact is an escalation event.
