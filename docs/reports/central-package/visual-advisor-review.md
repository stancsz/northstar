## Inspected scope and observations

Read repository instructions, direction, goal/handoff, Northstar, Codex QA, Codex Subagents and role profiles. Inspected the changed Advisor instructions and metadata, central routing, operating-system guidance, English/Chinese README, installation notes and final evaluation entry against the owner's request. Inspected caller/reader source without invoking a provider.

- A small-model UI, illustration or generated-image task must obtain Advisor direction before choosing its visual approach; routine visual work cannot use the ordinary no-blocker/no-consult shortcut. Actual image delivery and inspection are required, rather than a worker summary, source, path, OCR or successful build.
- An illegible screenshot requires an overall view and multiple labeled original-resolution crops. The Advisor chooses additional regions, zooms, states, references and comparisons. Outstanding requests remain mandatory within existing authority; inaccessible evidence leaves the judgment unverified.
- Feedback must identify visible regions, prioritized defects, concrete repairs and the next judging view. Vague advice requires another image-backed question. The worker returns repaired screenshots and relevant earlier/reference views to the same Advisor and repeats requested inspection. One opinion or a claimed repair cannot complete the loop.
- Ordinary blocker prerequisites, two-call advice and three-call reader ceilings explicitly do not truncate required visual consultation. The visual Astra escalation explicitly does not require a system-level blocker. Existing authority, resource and failed-attempt recovery limits still apply; extra opinions do not reset them or waive an unmet criterion.
- An Advisor that directs/coauthors a design cannot independently accept it. Existing QA rules retain builder ownership of repairs and separate nonauthor acceptance; required human aesthetic approval remains separate.
- Missing image-capable transport is handled honestly: `ask_expert.py` constructs text message content; the reader snapshot accepts specified UTF-8 source types and excludes binary/image files. The bundled helpers cannot establish screenshot review. The new rule requires an available authorized image channel and leaves the required consultation unverified when absent, without substituting the worker gateway or changing service configuration.
