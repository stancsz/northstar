# One handoff list both sides can check

## Rental companies buy workspace access

Record the rental items, quantities, agreed time and place, and current status. Staff check the physical items and mark them prepared; the organizer can view the same handoff summary.

**Intended outcome:** replace “I thought you had it” with a shared checklist. Whether this prevents misses is untested.

# Add people on site only when needed

## Organizers may separately buy staffed delivery

For Riverside Market, the organizer may purchase **staffed delivery and on-site handoff** separately—or keep their existing pickup or transport plan. It is not required with workspace access.

- Rental team: prepare and confirm the rental list
- Optional delivery staff: deliver as agreed and check items over with the organizer
- Organizer: confirm receipt; log issues and contact the rental team

**Scope, price, and availability need validation; no promise of punctuality or zero errors.**

# First, show us how you work today

## Then decide if StageKit is worth testing

1. What do you use today to manage rental lists, changes, and handoffs? Which step causes the most friction?
2. Who would use this workspace? Who packs, delivers, and signs off? What would be missing for you to use it?
3. If this workspace fit your rental business, what would it be worth per operating location per month? Give an amount and currency first—**zero is valid**.

**We’ll hear your unaided estimate before discussing any price range.** A hypothetical answer is not a purchase commitment.
