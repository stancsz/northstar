## Cold-reader pass: defects and fixes

### Bicycle repair approvals

- First draft already showed a coherent repair journey, itemized math ($48 + $72 + $35 = $155), customer authorization boundary, and an optional in-context assistant with reviewed sending and cautious local-inference limits.
- Defect: the closing slide asked about current workflow and unaided price but did not state a concrete pilot unit/deliverable or explicitly ask about alternatives.
- Additional first/revised-pass defect found in parent inspection: Chinese translated brand and shop names, making identity drift across language versions; initial dollar notation also omitted currency.
- Fix in revised EN/ZH: retain **Spoke & Sprocket** and **North Loop Cycles** exactly; make all sample prices explicit CAD (C$48 + C$72 + C$35 = C$155 CAD). Revised closing slide proposes one shop, one shared estimate link, reviewed messages, approval history; asks about tools/alternatives and preserves unaided amount, zero, and currency. Does not imply the pilot or software exists.

### Wholesale coffee reorder

- First draft defects: unsubstantiated automatic supplier submission and “hours every week” savings; unverified live catalog and confirmed delivery; fixed $39/month anchor before unaided willingness-to-pay; “guarantees fewer stockouts”; checkout screen implied a finished product.
- Fix in revised EN/ZH: human-created and human-sent order through the existing channel; supplier email/cutoff are manually checked; catalog/integration and savings are explicitly unverified; removes price anchor and guarantee; asks unaided per-café monthly amount including zero and currency, after current workflow/alternatives; labels pilot as proposed.
- Bilingual identity defect: initial drafts translated **BeanRoute** and names such as **Juniper Café** and **Mei** into different proper names. Revised Chinese retains the English names (Chinese glosses may accompany them) to match the shared-screen identity.
- Internal consistency checked: scenario is Juniper Café, Mei, Harborlight beans; 4 on hand vs 12 par; proposed 12 bags = 8 espresso + 4 decaf; estimate $264 CAD. No unit price breakdown is invented.

### Institution coaching and participant subscriptions

- First draft defects: service promise omitted who pays versus who receives service; “subscription” value and affordability were unsupported; dashboard privacy boundary was ambiguous; claimed improved wellbeing/adherence without evidence; vague “contact us to enroll” omitted buyer discovery and price elicitation.
- Fix in revised EN/ZH: clarifies that the institution pays a proposed cohort-delivery fee and each participant separately pays a subscription fee for service access; these are separate unpriced hypotheses. Names weekly human coach and practical non-clinical exercises; distinguishes individual coaching notes from institution-level enrollment/attendance; removes efficacy claim; makes capacity/privacy practices unestablished; asks current alternatives/users/payers and separately elicits unaided institution cohort fee and participant 90-day fee, each including zero and currency.
- Bilingual identity defect: initial Chinese versions translated **SteadySteps**, **Cedar House Community Centre**, **Alex**, and **Sam**. Revised Chinese preserves these English proper names with optional glosses.
- Internal consistency checked: fictional Cedar House Community Centre / 雪松社区中心, Alex / 亚历克斯, coach Sam / 萨姆; 90 days, weekly sessions; dashboard sample 8 enrolled / 6 attended this week. No diagnosis, treatment, or health outcome claim.
