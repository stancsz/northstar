## Test the fit before discussing a price
Concept only; no live supplier connection or proven savings. First, how do you count stock and reorder today, what tools and alternatives do you use, and who participates? What would you change? What would you pay per café each month, including zero, and in what currency? That is a hypothetical answer, not a commitment.
