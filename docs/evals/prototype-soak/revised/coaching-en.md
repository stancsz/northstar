# Turn 90 days into a supported routine, one week at a time
**SteadySteps** · Institution pays the cohort fee; participants separately subscribe for service access

`[MISSING IMAGE: shared screen 1 — fictional institution cohort offer and participant subscription enrollment]`

---

## Replace a handoff with a named weekly contact
Fictional **Cedar House Community Centre** pays a proposed 90-day cohort delivery fee for adults who opt in to building everyday routines. Each participant separately pays a subscription fee for their service access. A named coach meets each participant weekly and shares practical, non-clinical exercises. The two fee amounts and subscription terms are open questions.

`[MISSING IMAGE: shared screen 2 — fictional cohort calendar with named coach and weekly session slots]`

---

## Choose one small action together
Participant Alex picks a routine goal, discusses barriers with coach Sam, and chooses one action for the week. Alex can change the goal; Sam adapts activities to Alex’s preferences. This is coaching, not diagnosis or treatment.

`[MISSING IMAGE: shared screen 3 — fictional participant goal, coach notes visible only to participant and coach, action choice]`

---

## Give each party the view they need
Alex sees their own plan and upcoming sessions. Cedar House’s coordinator sees cohort-level enrollment and attendance to arrange staffing; individual coaching notes stay out of the institutional summary. The sample dashboard shows 8 enrolled and 6 attended this week.

## Test the service model before buying
Proposed pilot: one 90-day cohort, weekly human coaching, participant subscription, and a coordinator summary. Price, capacity, privacy practices, and outcomes are not established; no health improvement is claimed. First, how do you support routines today, what alternatives do participants use, and who pays or participates? What would you change? What would your institution pay for cohort delivery, including zero, and in what currency? Separately, what would a participant pay for 90-day access, including zero, and in what currency? Hypothetical only.
