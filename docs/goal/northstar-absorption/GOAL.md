# Goal: decide what external practices Northstar should absorb next

Date: 2026-10-22. Status: **decision complete; real-task evidence remains the next project proof.** The owner wants Northstar to keep producing useful outcomes by selectively learning from other projects.

## Outcome and acceptance

- **Outcome:** make an evidence-based, prioritized recommendation about which practices to absorb, defer, or reject.
- **Acceptance:** inspect Northstar's current direction and evaluation evidence; independently inventory existing mechanisms; use the requested Codex Advisor Gemini/Sol council; distinguish source fact, inference, and unverified effect; identify one next action that can change the recommendation.
- **Constraint:** do not add another operating framework or claim efficacy based on admired-project descriptions, synthetic checks, or an advisor opinion.

## Ownership and evidence

- Orchestrator: primary agent, accountable for decision and integration.
- Worker: `/root/northstar_absorption_scan`; report: [current-mechanism inventory](../../reports/northstar-absorption/inventory.md).
- Advisor: configured Gemini 4 Argon then GPT-6.1 Sol council; receipt: [council response and usage](../../evals/northstar-absorption-council.json).
- Governing Northstar evidence: [next-proof brief](../../northstar/next-proof.md), [dogfood evaluation](../../evals/dogfood-review.md), and [operating-system evaluation](../../evals/operating-system.md).

## Decision

**Absorb no new mechanisms now.** Prioritize execution of the already-specified matched real-task cohort, preserving the current acceptance bar and accounting for total effort and owner rescue. Keep the task-changeset SCM design as an unpiloted protocol; implement or operationalize it only if a real task demonstrates a specific handoff, overlap, or stale-review failure. Defer a Smartlog-style view until a real discoverability/coordination problem is observed. Reject a monorepo mandate, Scion-scale server/storage architecture, duplicate task graph/ledger, global lock, and new approval layers without demonstrated need.

This is a recommendation and sequencing decision, not evidence that Northstar already outperforms alternatives or that the deferred mechanisms have no value. The matched cohort has not been run, no qualifying software tasks were selected in this decision, and its three cases may not surface concurrent-work failures.

## Acceptance record

- Independent inventory confirms that Northstar already encodes owner-goal and acceptance alignment, bounded authority and one-writer scopes, independent QA, durable recovery, checkpoint learning, and proportionate process.
- Existing evidence establishes limited documentation and synthetic/local behaviors only. Real accepted-work improvement, complete-effort economics, owner rescue, real business outcomes, and comparative superiority remain unverified.
- Gemini recommended the real-task cohort before new frameworks while warning that three tasks could miss concurrency friction. Sol independently agreed on sequencing and clarified that observed overlap/interruption failures could warrant a targeted pilot of the already-designed SCM safeguards.
- The recommendation matches the existing `docs/northstar/next-proof.md`; no new runtime, skill rule, tracking system, or architecture was added.
