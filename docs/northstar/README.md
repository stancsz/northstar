# Northstar direction

## Purpose

Help an owner turn intent into useful projects, products and business operations that meet their standards, while reducing routine supervision and inspecting actual value and operating cost.

The target user is an owner or developer working with AI agents who needs initiative and reliable completion. The pain is convenient shortcuts: an agent appears finished while integration, UX, failure handling, or other essential work is missing.

## Confirmed owner expectations

- Keep the package native to Codex and usable by other agents, including Claude Code and OpenCode. Installation and discovery must explicitly say that `Codex` names do not restrict who may use the skills. Preserve names, adapt host capabilities, and keep service prerequisites honest. See [installation guidance](../misc/install.md#installing-from-another-agent).

- Maximize useful accepted outcomes with the least elapsed time and total tokens, including rework. Lean first, functioning first: the leanest, cleanest product, no spaghetti code, maximum practical reuse, and top-class design that stays inexpensive to extend. Remove unnecessary machinery and duplication; keep wording as short as the task allows. Preserve readability and credible extension paths without speculative frameworks or unmeasured savings claims. See [design guidance](../../skills/northstar/SKILL.md#excellent-design-with-minimal-machinery).
- Deliver a usable MVP quickly, then build on accepted work in small increments. Bound each delivery, reuse applicable evidence, and change approach when retries stop producing information. Long-term quality requirements remain visible without making every future feature a prerequisite for the current slice. See the [delivery rules](../../skills/northstar/SKILL.md#deliver-a-usable-mvp-then-improve-in-increments).
- Challenge the customer, problem, commercial value, and production direction early. Research facts independently and ask for decisions the owner actually needs to make.
- Use reference products to resolve specific design questions or evaluate a requested comparison. Name the workflows and observable criteria; do not impose default parity targets or invent overall quality percentages.
- Write concise instructions that tell agents what to do, when to repeat work, when to ask, and when to finish. Express quality standards as inspectable behavior rather than slogans.
- Preserve existing authorization across context changes and delegation; do not require approval to continue already-requested work. Managing agents resolve ordinary decisions, dependencies, overlap and stalls, then inspect the integrated workflow. Use short [role briefs and a coordination loop](../../skills/codex-subagents/SKILL.md#coordinate-to-a-working-result); status relay alone is insufficient. Platform permission blocks remain distinct from missing user authorization.
- The owner grants the commander standing decision authority for the assigned project/business outcome within existing targets and resource limits, including bounded subdelegation. The commander supplies actionable guidance and resolves remaining in-mandate product/value tradeoffs by expected useful value, full cost, time, downside and reversibility. Do not return ordinary or consequential covered choices to the owner for repeated approval. Once Notion is configured for that work, use its existing connection for covered project reading, creation, updates and coordination without per-page/per-action permission questions. Carry the grant forward; diagnose real tool-access failures as dependencies rather than asking for authority already given. The owner's "99% guidance" expresses responsibility for thorough direction and minimal human intervention, not a measured success or autonomy rate. See [commander authority](../../skills/northstar/SKILL.md#ask-only-for-decisions-the-user-owns).
- Balance autonomy with coordination: keep outcome, evidence, data, explicit limits and authorization firm; delegate internal implementation choices. Coordinate shared interfaces with affected owners and escalate unresolved exceptions. Scale records, management layers and review to actual dependency/risk; remove temporary oversight after its cause is resolved. See [proportionate process](../../skills/northstar/SKILL.md#match-process-to-the-work).
- Keep the system flexible and reduce bureaucracy: activate functions for real needs, combine compatible roles, use direct asynchronous coordination and review a coherent delivery rather than every internal action. Reuse a qualified nonauthor reviewer and its scoped evidence across management roles. Consolidate records and remove empty meetings or temporary layers once their purpose ends. Retain independent acceptance, meaningful checkpoint learning and authority boundaries; lighter process must not become self-certification.
- Reuse each target project's existing records rather than imposing Northstar folders. Default to direct implementation and verification; add continuation, delegation and QA only as the task requires. This repository retains its explicit AGENTS.md layout.
- Recover stalled work using a persistent checkpoint and bounded investigation: count evidence-backed progress against the unmet criterion across agents and context changes. Validate recovery guidance with scoped execution trials, while distinguishing those trials from long-session reliability or enforced limits.
- Require initiative after exhausted recovery: decide and execute an authorized alternative, resolve a dependency, or stop the failed route and advance independent work. Delegate the intended effect and decision authority together. Managers own remedies rather than forwarding status; preserve acceptance, inherited history and authority. See the [decision rule](../../skills/northstar/SKILL.md#decide-after-exhausted-recovery). This is an inspectable practice, not a claim of a perfect or self-enforcing system.
- Adapt useful cross-domain experience from historical texts, modern military discussion, companies and startup/VC practice: focus delivery constraints, delegate reversible decisions, test assumptions before increasing commitment, contain defects, leave capacity to finish, and learn from honest failures. Keep the [source mapping and limits](../../skills/northstar/references/operating-principles.md) separate from concise execution rules. Shorten decision paths without expanding authority. Retain necessary alignment, debate, expert consultation and retrospectives; remove empty hierarchy and copied budget formulas; sampled organizational advice is not proof of agent efficacy.
- Use the [operating system](../../skills/northstar/references/operating-system.md) for project and business management: strategy-to-work alignment, accountable local initiative, reasoned dissent with a superior-chaired meeting when unresolved, bounded expert consultation, operating reviews and transfer of responsibility. Distill useful mechanisms rather than transplanting entire military or MBA taxonomies.
- Validate with capable lower-cost execution models and stronger direction/decision capability when warranted. Record actual model roles and intervention costs; all-strong-model tests do not establish economical operation. Missing token or price data stays unknown.
- Assign [functional profiles](../../skills/codex-subagents/references/role-profiles.md) for needed product/outcome, architecture, building/integration, independent QA, experience/taste, operations and expertise. Combine compatible functions; never let a creator independently test, certify or judge its own deliverable. Builder development checks and solo learning remain useful but do not replace a separate reviewer. Manager repairs inherit the same conflict; unavailable independent review leaves acceptance unverified. Preserve authority, artifact identity and recovery through [agent handoffs](../../skills/codex-subagents/references/agent-handoffs.md). These are Markdown practices, not A2A runtime support.
- Critics and verifiers use the owner's standards and inspect actual results. Fix shortcuts and recheck rather than merely listing defects.
- Use concise QA to prevent sloppy delivery: plan visible work, inspect rendered results and real workflows, challenge readiness with relevant failure cases, and verify repairs. Distinguish material defects from preference changes, missing evidence from a pass, and AI review from human approval. See [Codex QA](../../skills/codex-qa/SKILL.md).
- Visible deliveries need screenshots actually viewed, concrete visual observations, and executed functional checks; a bare LGTM is insufficient. Keep routine QA fast with representative evidence and targeted rechecks, expanding only for requirements or observed risk.
- Art, image inspection and aesthetic decisions require hands-on Advisor guidance, especially for small models and workhorses with weak taste. Actively attach screenshot pixels to the consultation with `--image`, fulfill its requested crops/details, obtain concrete fixes and return repaired views for feedback. Enable scoped reader mode when source uncertainty is plausible so the Advisor can decide to dispatch its own source reader. Follow [the mandatory visual loop](../../skills/codex-advisor/SKILL.md#mandatory-visual-and-aesthetic-guidance), which overrides ordinary consultation triggers/call ceilings without waiving independent or human acceptance.
- Identify Luna/workhorse capability risks from primary evidence beyond visual taste and consult a compact Advisor early, then again at distinct risky decisions or changed evidence. Use the [dated risk register](../../skills/codex-advisor/references/capability-triggers.md), separating direct model comparisons from general limitations and prudent routing. Required risk guidance overrides ordinary consultation ceilings; denials, source-outage disclosure, resource limits, recovery and independent acceptance remain binding. Higher capability alone is no guarantee of safer judgment.
- This package's Advisor is fixed to GPT-6.1 Sol, including visual and source-reader follow-ups. Remove Astra escalation and older Sol selection from the caller and dedicated service; an unavailable 6.1 route fails visibly without substitution. Historical comparative model results remain research evidence.
- Ship four distinct skills together: `northstar`, `codex-qa`, `codex-subagents` and `codex-advisor`. Install them as siblings in one operation; `$northstar` is the central coordinator and each companion remains directly invocable. Keep relative links inside the four-skill bundle and load companions only when needed. Codex Advisor replaces the former Luna Advisor skill from Subroute; keep its caller/reader and tests together, and leave a source redirect. Gateway runtime remains in Subroute.
- Keep the operating methods as Markdown. The explicitly migrated advisor helpers are a narrow exception, not a reason to add executable enforcement or reporting machinery.
- Treat the [self-learning loop](../../skills/northstar/SKILL.md#learn-from-every-use) as a core responsibility: at every meaningful checkpoint, inspect what worked/failed, check against intent and acceptance, persist scoped lessons and use them before the next action. Solo agents review their own learning without treating it as independent QA or acceptance; teams resolve evidence and decisions through a short review of existing handoffs. Preserve rejected paths and reopening conditions, supersede stale advice, and carry experience across context changes without resetting recovery. Recording alone is insufficient; required reviews must produce evidence, decisions and next actions. Do not claim guaranteed drift prevention.
- Separately recommend useful contributions and route skill problems to [Northstar Issues](https://github.com/stancsz/northstar/issues), with sanitized evidence, deduplication, inherited publishing authority, and verified submission status. See the [feedback practice](../../skills/northstar/references/skill-feedback.md). Local learning does not depend on public posting.
- Use `docs/northstar/`, `docs/goal/`, `docs/reports/`, and `docs/evals/` as shared memory. Keep only subdirectories directly under `docs/`; other supporting documentation belongs in `docs/misc/`. Keep a useful `AGENTS.md`, scoped scratch work in `tmp/`, appropriate ignore rules, and meaningful commits.
- Keep total project artifacts below 100 GB across agents, including ignored and project-attributable working data.
- Use explicit ownership: the orchestrator owns and uses `docs/northstar/` and is accountable for the integrated product outcome; supervisors own and use their `docs/goal/<goal>/GOAL.md` and index entries; workers produce work and write `docs/reports/<goal>/<task>.md`. Supervisors read reports and inspect artifacts before task acceptance. Critics and verifiers provide independent review where available. See the [role boundaries](../../skills/northstar/SKILL.md#ownership-north-star--goals--tasks).

## Product assumptions and open questions

The [dogfood review's next-proof brief](next-proof.md) proposes the next validation sequence: measure real accepted work, total effort and owner rescue with the frozen package, then simplify observed friction and validate business operations separately. This is proposed work; the pilot has not run. The existing owner expectations remain in force.

The above requirements come from the owner's instructions in this project. Demand beyond this use case, a commercial model for Northstar itself, and comparative performance against other engineering skills have not been validated. No leading equivalent has yet been selected through a comparative evaluation. Do not claim profitability or competitive parity without that work.

Goal-Driven Engineering supplies the goal-oriented practice adapted by Northstar; this methodological source is not evidence of competitive superiority.
