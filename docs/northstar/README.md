# Northstar direction

Current priority: make the existing delivery loop work reliably before adding capabilities. Follow the canonical [delivery contract](../../skills/northstar/SKILL.md#delivery-contract), not a collection of parallel initiatives. The [delivery-focus goal](../goal/delivery-focus/GOAL.md) owns this commitment; older proposed mechanisms remain background until a concrete delivery need justifies them.

## Purpose

Help an owner turn intent into useful projects, products and business operations that meet their standards, while reducing routine supervision and inspecting actual value and operating cost.

Develop this package as concise, reusable skills first. Keep application implementations, interfaces, deployment plans and application-specific records in their own repositories.

The target user is an owner or developer working with AI agents who needs initiative and reliable completion. The pain is convenient shortcuts: an agent appears finished while integration, UX, failure handling, or other essential work is missing.

## Confirmed owner expectations

The owner's outcome is useful delivery to the agreed standard, with initiative inside the existing authority and evidence that distinguishes completed work from assumptions. The [delivery contract](../../skills/northstar/SKILL.md#delivery-contract) is the single operational source for the five coordinated core skills (`northstar`, `subagent-orchestration`, `independent-qa`, `advisor-council`, `task-completion-review`) and their templates; [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) holds repository-specific boundaries.

## Product assumptions and open questions

The [delivery-focus repair](../evals/delivery-focus/README.md) shortened the central entrypoint and its matched constructed task pair completed on both arms. The revised arm reported 44,786 fewer input tokens in that one comparison, with one additional command; both emitted the skill-description-budget warning. This supports scoped feasibility only, not general effectiveness or cost claims. Further workflow changes should be driven by observed failures on real accepted work.

The [dogfood review's next-proof brief](next-proof.md) proposes measuring real accepted work, total effort and owner rescue. That pilot has not run. Prefer one representative real delivery as the next effectiveness check; do not open another framework or process goal before it.

The [product-delivery goal](../goal/product-delivery/GOAL.md) applies the owner's production/sellable-outcome request: prevent premature full-goal closure, preserve release obligations and obtain real delivery evidence. Skills-only cleanup and passing synthetic cases do not complete that broader outcome; its real-product proof remains open.

The above requirements come from the owner's instructions in this project. Demand beyond this use case, a commercial model for Northstar itself, and comparative performance against other engineering skills have not been validated. No leading equivalent has yet been selected through a comparative evaluation. Do not claim profitability or competitive parity without that work.

Goal-Driven Engineering supplies the goal-oriented practice adapted by Northstar; this methodological source is not evidence of competitive superiority.
